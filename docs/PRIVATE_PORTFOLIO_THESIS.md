# Private portfolio thesis evidence

The existing Portfolio Doctor remains the authenticated, owner-scoped deterministic risk review. A separate machine-to-machine selection endpoint resolves active holdings to unambiguous public registry ISINs, returning only exchange and ISIN (maximum 200). The private local research cache is a separate SQLite file on the existing volume; public discovery snapshots and public news alerts never read it. The existing authorized Upstox company and news collectors run on those identities with their permission checks, bounded requests and cooldowns. This means Upstox can observe requested public instrument identities; no account IDs, holding amounts, quantities, cost basis or user notes are sent by this path. Disable ATLAS_PORTFOLIO_THESIS_ENABLED to stop the new selection/collection path. BSE news remains unavailable without validated mapping.

The authenticated Spring review response joins only the current owner's already resolved ISINs to the cached evidence through a token-protected internal Python endpoint. Reads do not make provider/model calls. At most 100 equity identities and 20 exact saved mutual fund schemes are enriched per response. Exact scheme NAV history reuses existing AMFI/MFAPI metrics. Missing evidence stays missing; existing risk flags and notification snapshots are unchanged.

Local AI uses the existing grounded model router with providers restricted to OLLAMA, explicit configured model and budgets, and no external fallback. Only allowlisted public ratio names/numeric values and bounded news-topic counts are supplied; no portfolio fields, headlines, user text or broker tokens. Reviews are keyed to evidence and model, expire after 24 hours, and are limited to one per scheduler pass. Existing citation/type and unsafe-action checks apply; they do not prove model correctness. Model output is optional commentary, never a trade instruction or full thesis verdict. No new AI service is enabled automatically.

Limits: bounded selection and existing historical availability; no official fund holdings, TER or benchmark, no intrinsic valuation, verified sentiment, comprehensive portfolio-wide AI allocation advice or live acceptance. The private cache contains public evidence selected from held identities and is protected by local filesystem/container access; it is not a separately encrypted owner vault. No real account data was accessed during implementation.

## Automatic held mutual fund history

The existing permission-gated fund history scheduler now prioritizes up to two distinct exact held AMFI scheme codes each pass before the five discovery-category slots. It covers registered regular, debt, and other schemes outside Direct Growth discovery categories, provided a recent AMFI NAV exists. It reuses the same persisted claim/cooldown and MFAPI adapter; no new provider, model, or credential is required. Only public scheme codes are selected for provider requests; no owners, quantities, costs or account identifiers are transmitted. The provider can observe the requested public scheme identities. Duplicate held codes across owners are deduplicated by the catalog query and shared attempt ledger. Existing exact-scheme evidence is available on the next review read once enough validated history is collected. Coverage remains bounded, permission-gated and dependent on source availability; this is not a complete investment thesis or a validated forward recommendation.

## Decision-oriented portfolio digest

The authenticated review response now includes a deterministic `thesis_summary` for each holding, with recorded risk flags, sourced measured observations, review prompts and explicit evidence gaps. The dashboard shows this digest before detailed source reports and prioritizes holdings with review prompts. The aggregate `thesis_review_count` includes recorded risk flags and is not a new trading signal or score. No provider or model call is added by this synthesis.

Company comparisons accept recognized, nonduplicate ratio aliases with expected provider units, finite company/sector values and positive valuation/leverage ratios. Relative multiples and profitability are comparison prompts only: reporting periods, sector comparability and fair value remain unverified. News topics are prompts to open source headlines, not confirmed events. Exact-scheme fund observations reuse validated historical CAGR, maximum drawdown and weekly volatility when present; a negative CAGR or historical drawdown of at least 20% is flagged for review, not a forecast or recommendation. Missing evidence never implies portfolio health. Official filings, benchmark, expenses, fund holdings overlap and forward strategy validation remain outstanding.
