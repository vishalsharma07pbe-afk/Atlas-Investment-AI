## Integrated daily briefing
The existing overview endpoint includes a deterministic summary of current saved research only. Stale/failed/disabled sections do not count as current. Equity watch counts are not qualified recommendations; full qualification remains unavailable. Fund growth picks are distinct from historical watches and pending categories. Institutional directions use existing paired five-date evidence, not a forecast. No new external call, private holding payload, or separate scheduler. The existing portfolio summary and detailed research views remain in place. This is a partial vertical improvement to Phase E, not a completed AI briefing or final recommendation engine. The next substantive work must connect dated stock/fund/portfolio evidence to actual qualification and complete reports, and continue Phase B source reliability where it blocks them.

## Release decision
Require all applicable original acceptance criteria: actual automatic data refresh and portfolio monitoring, autonomous stock/fund discovery, grounded AI, meaningful conclusions and reassessment conditions, automatic alerts, truthful failures, clean result-first UI, privacy, restart stability, green exact-release CI, independent authorized-data acceptance, no trade execution, and documented evidence/blockers. Do not claim production completeness from a passing synthetic run. Do not delete existing volumes. Review and complete related work as cohesive vertical phases.

## Workstream B — durable autonomous research orchestration
B01 partial: replace the previous serial all-or-nothing polling pass with a SQLite job ledger for seven independently scheduled snapshot sections and seven auxiliary research engines. Existing section saves, provider-specific claims/quotas, public/private cache separation and research routes remain intact. The dispatcher prioritizes due portfolio work, schedules oldest due jobs fairly, gates public company/news/replay/AI work on current equity evidence and configured permissions, and still evaluates previously published mature outcomes during discovery failure. Each claim is transactional, limited to one active job per database, has a 30-minute expiry for interrupted attempts, and rejects late completion by a superseded claim. Failure retry starts at 60 seconds, doubles up to configured cadence; missing evidence waits 60 seconds. Normal shutdown waits only for the currently running job; existing provider I/O limits still apply. On restart, completed cadence persists and interrupted work is eligible after lease expiry; this is not immediate crash recovery, distributed exactly-once execution or transactional fencing of provider side effects. The existing source-readiness screen includes job state, last completed pass, next due, retry and interruption counts; no private holdings, provider error bodies or secrets are persisted in the coordinator. PASS_COMPLETED means a function returned; actual source freshness and provider-specific failure states remain independently authoritative. Regression tests exercise persistence, expiry, stale claim rejection, independent failure handling and permission/dependency gates. CI and populated local acceptance pending. B02/B03/B04/B05/B06 remain outstanding; this does not unify existing Java ingestion schedules or establish verified market-calendar scheduling, provider reconnection or adjusted history.
