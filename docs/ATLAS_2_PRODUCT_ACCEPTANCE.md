## Integrated daily briefing
The existing overview endpoint includes a deterministic summary of current saved research only. Stale/failed/disabled sections do not count as current. Equity watch counts are not qualified recommendations; full qualification remains unavailable. Fund growth picks are distinct from historical watches and pending categories. Institutional directions use existing paired five-date evidence, not a forecast. No new external call, private holding payload, or separate scheduler. The existing portfolio summary and detailed research views remain in place. This is a partial vertical improvement to Phase E, not a completed AI briefing or final recommendation engine. The next substantive work must connect dated stock/fund/portfolio evidence to actual qualification and complete reports, and continue Phase B source reliability where it blocks them.

## Release decision
Require all applicable original acceptance criteria: actual automatic data refresh and portfolio monitoring, autonomous stock/fund discovery, grounded AI, meaningful conclusions and reassessment conditions, automatic alerts, truthful failures, clean result-first UI, privacy, restart stability, green exact-release CI, independent authorized-data acceptance, no trade execution, and documented evidence/blockers. Do not claim production completeness from a passing synthetic run. Do not delete existing volumes. Review and complete related work as cohesive vertical phases.

## Integrated stock research conclusions
Current technical-watch cards now carry deterministic per-stock conclusions, evidence status, retained qualification blockers, risk warnings and reassessment conditions. Existing historical replay rotates through the expanded discovery universe without selection by current watch rank; the cached result attaches only on matching registry ID, venue, symbol and underlying observation date. Existing AI review hashes include explicitly allowlisted reported annual trend comparisons and compatible same-stock replay cost metrics when available. The scheduler attaches cached evidence before requesting a review; the browser read path uses the same evidence hash. External providers remain opt-in, paid providers remain separately gated, and private holdings and untrusted prose are excluded. This integrates part of Phases C/D/E; it does not constitute full qualification, fair-value estimation, verified news sentiment, committee consensus, portfolio-level backtesting, or validated entry/exit levels. Actual provider evidence and local acceptance remain outstanding.

## Workstream C — fundamental quality and relative valuation
C02/C03/C04/C05 (partial): normalized provider evidence now feeds a shared deterministic fundamental assessment for both discovery candidates and held companies. Matched consecutive FY and same-unit annual trends support net margin, operating cash/profit and reported liabilities/assets comparisons. No new provider, schema migration, manual input or external AI payload. Does not establish fair value, business quality, market share or current ratio dates. Dated valuation inputs, independent filing verification, peer/industry evidence, actual authorized source coverage and full qualification remain outstanding. CI and local acceptance pending for this change.

No scenario price or investment recommendation is manufactured.
