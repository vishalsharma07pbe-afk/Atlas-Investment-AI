# Prospective research reliability

The existing watch publication ledger now preserves the exact public ISIN where available, and maturity evaluation rejects identity changes. Older publications are not rewritten. The automatic overview summarizes the current technical methodology's adjusted-price publications over the preceding year, with a bounded 5,000-row scan per horizon. A mature window includes seven days of exit-date allowance. The earliest publication per exact ISIN is selected before looking at outcomes, with overlapping windows excluded even when an earlier outcome is missing. Unresolved or incompatible identities and methodologies, incomplete windows and invalid records are counted explicitly.

Descriptive cohort thresholds require at least 30 observed outcomes, 10 distinct observed ISINs, 80% outcome coverage and no scan truncation. These illustrative thresholds are not statistical proof. Cost scenarios apply 15/30/60 bps per side to recorded adjusted close-to-close changes; no actual execution, fees, index benchmarks, dividends or independent probability calibration is claimed. A nonpositive mean under any scenario flags cost sensitivity. The prospective sample remains distinct from retrospective rule replay.

Current equity watches retain all existing research blockers. Additional checks surface observation freshness, raw-price adjustment gaps, missing company/news evidence, reported operating deterioration, regulatory headline topics and prospective evidence limitations. Headline topics and provider financial comparisons are review prompts, not verified negative events. No candidate is promoted to a recommendation, no probability or trade instruction is generated, and no AI/provider call or portfolio data is added. Intraday and mutual fund reliability require separate evidence. Live outcome coverage will depend on future mature publications; older records lacking ISIN cannot be included in this cohort. CI and live acceptance remain required.

The frozen score ledger now also evaluates fixed calendar publication quarters only after the entire quarter's horizon maturity plus seven-day allowance. It gives equal weight to publication dates with at least 80% outcome coverage, requires 30 observations, 10 distinct ISINs, 10 covered publication dates and 80% overall observation/date coverage per band, and compares unchanged high vs low score bands in common quarters. At least two comparable quarters are required for descriptive temporal comparison. No cutoffs are fitted; dates are not necessarily matched between bands and cross-sectional dependence remains. This does not establish statistical significance or calibrate probabilities.
