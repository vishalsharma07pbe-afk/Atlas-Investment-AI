# Automatic company news and event observations

Reuses the existing Upstox news adapter, public watch registry identity and autonomous scheduler. Compose configures the feature, gated by existing rights, entitlement and operator token. Select at most ten NSE watch candidates per batch, one request per 15 minutes at most, with 30-minute successful and one-hour failed retry cooldown. BSE mappings are not guessed. Provider-returned instrument associations are preserved even if the same article belongs to several stocks. Unknown response keys, future or over-72-hour articles, malformed and unsafe links, missing headlines and duplicate articles are excluded. No article fetching, raw provider response storage, private holdings, external AI or new paid service.

Current cards and overview show source-linked headlines and publication times. A bounded keyword match identifies earnings, regulatory, corporate-action, transaction and management topics for review; no NLP sentiment, event confirmation, causal price impact or recommendation qualification. Missing or failed provider responses are distinguished from an explicit empty result. Cache older than two hours is excluded. News topic alerts are converted to the existing public bell contract with stable source/date hashes, deduplicated across companies, at most 30 recent alerts. No new external channels; existing local and IPO alerts remain separate and preserved. Recent news cache is local; bell read state uses existing local browser behavior, not owner-specific notification history.

Global macro/news coverage, full company universe and BSE mapping, independently verified corporate filings, validated sentiment and event-impact research remain outstanding. No live provider acceptance was performed. This change does not modify the strict investment recommendation gates.
