# Valuation research

The cash-stream sensitivity uses four consecutive matched consolidated fiscal years of Upstox net profit and operating cash flow with exact source identity and coherent retrieval timestamp. Negative/nonpositive annual profit or cash blocks scenarios. The historical cash anchor is the lower of latest operating cash and four-year mean. Fixed illustrative assumptions (cash fraction 30/50/70%, growth -5/5/10%, discount 16/12/10%, terminal growth 0/3/4%) model a five-year hypothetical stream plus terminal perpetuity. These are not forecasts, WACC estimates, FCFE, FCFF, enterprise value, intrinsic equity value, or target prices. The terminal share is displayed to expose assumption sensitivity. No qualification gates change. Independently dated forward earnings, explicit capital expenditures, net debt, share basis, comparable unadjusted venue price and calibrated assumptions remain prerequisites. No numeric margin of safety is produced.
