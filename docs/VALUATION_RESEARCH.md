# Valuation research

The cash-stream sensitivity uses four consecutive matched consolidated fiscal years of Upstox net profit and operating cash flow with exact source identity and coherent retrieval timestamp. Negative/nonpositive annual profit or cash blocks scenarios. The historical cash anchor is the lower of latest operating cash and four-year mean. Fixed illustrative assumptions (cash fraction 30/50/70%, growth -5/5/10%, discount 16/12/10%, terminal growth 0/3/4%) model a five-year hypothetical stream plus terminal perpetuity. These are not forecasts, WACC estimates, FCFE, FCFF, enterprise value, intrinsic equity value, or target prices. The terminal share is displayed to expose assumption sensitivity. No qualification gates change. Independently dated forward earnings, explicit capital expenditures, net debt, share basis, comparable unadjusted venue price and calibrated assumptions remain prerequisites. No numeric margin of safety is produced.

The matrix holds cash fraction at 50% and terminal growth at 3%, varying only annual growth (-5/0/5/10%) and discount (10/12/14/16%). The current request timestamp, not capture time, controls the 24-hour freshness gate. Mutual fund views expose original-weight coverage and deterioration only, not weighted hypothetical present values.

## Prospective earnings baseline
The scheduler freezes next-quarter revenue and net-profit growth-persistence experiments for exact public selected ISINs only, using same-quarter year-ago bases and the latest reported YoY growth. Both bases must be positive; observed growth outside -50% to 100% is withheld rather than clipped. The publication must precede target quarter end. Frozen records are immutable; evaluate only later coherent reported quarter captures, requiring original bases unchanged. Compare absolute error as percentage points of the original seasonal base against no-growth baseline. Results are grouped by target quarter only after a fixed 150-day reporting allowance. Missing and revised cases remain in the denominator. This is an experimental provider-reported baseline, not independent analyst consensus, financial forecast certification, EPS or a qualified recommendation. No network calls are added, no model is tuned, and no holdings data is used. The existing company cache must supply the subsequent report; companies leaving the sample can remain unresolved.
