# Legal and risk disclosures

ATLAS is research software. It is not a broker, exchange, portfolio manager, research analyst, tax adviser, or fiduciary. It does not execute trades. Outputs do not constitute an offer, solicitation, suitability determination, guaranteed forecast, or individualized investment recommendation.

Market prices, NAVs, fundamentals, news, institutional data, model output, and corporate actions may be delayed, incomplete, revised, incorrectly mapped, or unavailable. Historical performance and backtests are not reliable indicators of future results. Simulations include assumptions and may omit liquidity, taxes, slippage, fees, survivorship effects, and market impact.

AI output can be wrong even when citations are present. A citation establishes the evidence item used by ATLAS, not the truth or completeness of the conclusion. Users must verify material information with authorized primary sources and consult appropriately licensed professionals.

Provider terms, exchange licenses, redistribution rights, privacy duties, retention rules, and local financial regulations remain the deployer's responsibility. Do not enable a feed merely because credentials work. Do not send secrets, unnecessary personal data, or confidential portfolio data to an external AI provider.

Production operators must publish jurisdiction-specific privacy terms, terms of use, support contacts, incident notice procedures, accessibility contact, and regulatory disclosures approved by counsel before launch.
