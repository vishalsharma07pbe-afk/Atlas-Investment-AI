# ATLAS Mutual Fund Research Methodology

Version: `MF-RESEARCH-1.0.0`

ATLAS keeps fund discovery, historical strength, forward outlook, official risk disclosure and suitability separate. A discovery record never becomes a recommendation merely because it has a name and NAV.

## Top Performer gate

Active Direct Growth schemes require a verified official category, a fresh dated NAV, at least three years of dated NAV history, an official Riskometer, expense ratio, one- and three-year returns, peer percentile, benchmark hit rate, worst rolling return, maximum drawdown and volatility. Scoring weights are peer percentile 25%, benchmark hit rate 25%, worst rolling return 20%, drawdown 15%, volatility 10% and cost 5%.

Index funds are grouped by underlying index and require tracking difference, tracking error, expense ratio, benchmark identity and at least three years of history.

## Growth Outlook gate

Growth Outlook is independent from trailing returns. Active schemes require a portfolio disclosure no older than 60 days, at least 80% portfolio coverage, 70% earnings coverage, 60% valuation coverage, and sourced earnings, valuation, sector-driver, concentration and liquidity evidence. Weights are earnings 30%, valuation 25%, sector drivers 20%, concentration 15% and liquidity 10%.

Passive schemes require an identified underlying index, at least 70% index-outlook coverage, a recent portfolio/index constitution and tracking difference. Execution quality and index outlook remain separate.

## Suitability

Equity funds are flagged as poor fits for goals at or below 36 months. Mid- and small-cap funds are also flagged when the selected risk tolerance is below their volatility profile. Suitability flags do not guarantee that a longer horizon is appropriate.

## Ratings

- Official Riskometer: an AMC/SEBI disclosure. ATLAS never invents it.
- ATLAS research rating: deterministic A/B/C label derived only after a list's eligibility gate passes.
- Evidence confidence: coverage of required inputs, separate from fund quality.

No BUY/SELL instruction, target NAV or probability of profit is produced. Snapshots store the methodology version, filters, source-coverage state and a content hash for reproducibility. Missing inputs produce fewer picks or an empty list.
