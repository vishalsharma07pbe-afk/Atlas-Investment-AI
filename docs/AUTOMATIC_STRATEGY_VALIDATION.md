# Automatic historical strategy validation

The existing autonomous research scheduler now rotates through a bounded sample of current registered NSE/BSE equities independently of current watch rankings, up to one instrument per exchange each pass. It reuses the local completed daily candle endpoint, requesting up to 500 observations, and caches attempts for 24 hours per identity. Recent attempts (48 hours) and their outcome coverage are displayed in the existing horizon workspace. Disabled automation, stale observations, raw or mixed adjustment/source history, gaps greater than seven calendar days, invalid prices and insufficient windows are not counted as observed replay results.

The replay calls the existing versioned swing, short-term and long-term technical rules on historical prefixes. Indicators are causal; the signal is computed at a completed close, with hypothetical entry at the next observed session open and exit at the close after 10, 40 or 120 observed sessions respectively. The first 60% of data (at least 200 observations) is reserved as warmup/development context; no parameter fitting or tuning is performed. Positions do not overlap within each instrument/horizon. These are fixed test holding windows, not trading instructions. Three illustrative all-in cost scenarios (15, 30, 60 bps per side) include entry and exit friction. The report includes compounded simulated net return, marked-close maximum drawdown, positive/negative/flat completed position counts, win rate, mean return, same-asset gross buy-and-hold over the test window, and matched gross exposure return. No-signal and small-sample results are explicit.

This is historical fixed-rule replay, not independent forward validation: current registry survivorship, revised historical candles, incomplete session calendars, selection bias, corporate action adjustments, liquidity, market impact, actual fees/taxes and dividends are not independently resolved. The same-asset benchmark is not a market index or risk-adjusted benchmark. Per-instrument positions across horizons and instruments are not independent; no pooled portfolio return or calibrated probability is inferred. Intraday and mutual fund strategies are not covered. The existing prospective watch outcome tracking remains unchanged. Recommendation blockers remain in force. Live acceptance requires suitable adjusted validated OHLCV and further prospective, point-in-time and index-benchmarked evidence. No additional external providers, private portfolio inputs, orders or paid services are introduced.

The existing authorized Nifty 50 price-index history can additionally provide a gross index reference from the first hypothetical entry session open to the final holdout close. It is attached only on exact equality of observed stock/index dates in that interval, with provider-validated regular-session data and positive revisions; missing evidence remains unavailable. The signed net strategy minus gross price-index return is a descriptive percentage-point difference, not alpha or an executable index investment. The Nifty 50 reference is not a universal venue-specific benchmark or total-return index. Stored history and calendar coverage remain subject to authorized local acceptance and revisions.

Signal-date trend cohorts use the same simulated positions and cached price index. For each signal, require the 61 matching observed dates ending at the signal close. The trailing 60-observation close-to-close Nifty 50 price change is classified as rising (at least +5%), falling (at most -5%) or sideways, otherwise unknown if evidence is missing. No subsequent index close enters classification. Thresholds are illustrative and uncalibrated. Report per-position mean net change and outcome counts for each existing friction scenario, plus classified/total coverage and small-sample flags. Unknown or empty cohorts have no performance estimate. These cohorts are descriptive, not market forecasts, independent samples, full economic regimes or portfolio returns. No new provider call is required.

Repeated chronological fixed-rule validation partitions the existing holdout into up to three balanced consecutive windows, with at least two full horizon holding intervals required. Each window uses the same causal signal rules and starts from independent hypothetical cash; no exposure spans a boundary. The prior closing observation may serve as the next window's signal. Report separate returns, drawdown, same-stock reference and exact-date matched Nifty 50 price index where available, plus positive/negative/flat counts and best/worst observed window returns at each friction assumption. Empty-signal windows are shown without return estimates. With 500 observations the long-term horizon generally lacks two full 120-observation windows; do not fabricate repeatability. Continuous holdout results are unchanged. This is a fixed-rule historical robustness check, not refitting or independent forward validation; the experimental momentum-model walk-forward endpoint is unaffected.

Historical execution diagnostics retain all original simulated positions. For each fixed illustrative INR 100,000/500,000/1,000,000 entry notional, compare entry and gross-price-ratio-scaled exit values to 0.5% of both the corresponding 20 preceding observation median and realized full-day adjusted close-times-volume proxy. Zero-volume endpoints explicitly exceed the proxy gate; incomplete history stays unknown. These are retrospective diagnostics, not trade filters, executable quantities or verified exchange turnover. Separately use the existing 30 bps per-side sensitivity as a baseline and add illustrative 0/25/75 bps per side; report net return and drawdown without changing original cost results. Actual fees/taxes, spread, depth, impact, tick size, auction and fill evidence remain unresolved. No new data calls or private capital inputs.

The full bounded replay input (up to 500 observations) is also fingerprinted with the same strict canonical evidence checks used by the shared recent fingerprint. Compare local per-date digests only on overlapping dates with the previous same-ISIN capture. Revised results are displayed as historical research but not attached to current watches on that pass. The existing recorded corporate-action context must be present and consistent; any recorded crossing event withholds replay until independent adjustment reconciliation. Empty recorded context is not proof that no action occurred. The bounded cache is not a point-in-time archive, and current snapshot attachment still validates only the shared recent history, not all older observations after the last replay. No new provider calls or schema changes.

The automatic replay additionally requires exact agreement with the stored venue calendar from its first observation through yesterday. The bounded internal read covers 1,100 prior dates, distinguishes regular, closed, unknown and unsupported special sessions and uses existing holiday/weekend closure rules. Missing regular-session candles (including the trailing interval), unexpected closed-day observations, unknown or special days and unavailable calendar evidence withhold automated replay. No gap is imputed. Dashboard shows blocked samples and bounded dates. This is local stored-calendar continuity, not official historical certification or timestamp-level reconciliation. Existing technical watches and manual simulations are not replaced.

Recorded listing evidence is compared across the existing discovery registry and single-instrument candle response, including identity, status, dates and source/revision. Contradictory dates or changed identity context withhold replay. Unknown listing date or source provenance leaves descriptive historical research only and prevents attachment to current watches. Consistent dates do not verify historic universe membership. Results explicitly represent a current active-registry sample; survivorship and selection bias remain. Historical membership, delisted/suspended securities and publication-time evidence require separate authorized data.

Prospective watch outcomes now require matching exact ISIN, predeclared maturity and stored regular session dates from strictly after publication through the first regular session on/after maturity (within the existing seven-day limits). Missing or unknown session evidence is retried, never replaced by a later convenient candle. Reuse the full adjusted validated OHLCV/revision manifest for the exact interval. Completed outcomes retain a versioned fingerprint and count; local SQLite update triggers preserve publication and observed records. Legacy observations remain visible but cannot contribute to current reliability metrics; eligible mature legacy watches stay in the denominator. This remains descriptive close-to-close monitoring, not an executed trade, verified corporate-action history or a calibrated recommendation.
