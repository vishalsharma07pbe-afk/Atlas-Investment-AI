# Automatic historical strategy validation

The existing autonomous research scheduler now rotates through a bounded sample of current registered NSE/BSE equities independently of current watch rankings, up to one instrument per exchange each pass. It reuses the local completed daily candle endpoint, requesting up to 500 observations, and caches attempts for 24 hours per identity. Recent attempts (48 hours) and their outcome coverage are displayed in the existing horizon workspace. Disabled automation, stale observations, raw or mixed adjustment/source history, gaps greater than seven calendar days, invalid prices and insufficient windows are not counted as observed replay results.

The replay calls the existing versioned swing, short-term and long-term technical rules on historical prefixes. Indicators are causal; the signal is computed at a completed close, with hypothetical entry at the next observed session open and exit at the close after 10, 40 or 120 observed sessions respectively. The first 60% of data (at least 200 observations) is reserved as warmup/development context; no parameter fitting or tuning is performed. Positions do not overlap within each instrument/horizon. These are fixed test holding windows, not trading instructions. Three illustrative all-in cost scenarios (15, 30, 60 bps per side) include entry and exit friction. The report includes compounded simulated net return, marked-close maximum drawdown, positive/negative/flat completed position counts, win rate, mean return, same-asset gross buy-and-hold over the test window, and matched gross exposure return. No-signal and small-sample results are explicit.

This is historical fixed-rule replay, not independent forward validation: current registry survivorship, revised historical candles, incomplete session calendars, selection bias, corporate action adjustments, liquidity, market impact, actual fees/taxes and dividends are not independently resolved. The same-asset benchmark is not a market index or risk-adjusted benchmark. Per-instrument positions across horizons and instruments are not independent; no pooled portfolio return or calibrated probability is inferred. Intraday and mutual fund strategies are not covered. The existing prospective watch outcome tracking remains unchanged. Recommendation blockers remain in force. Live acceptance requires suitable adjusted validated OHLCV and further prospective, point-in-time and index-benchmarked evidence. No additional external providers, private portfolio inputs, orders or paid services are introduced.

The existing authorized Nifty 50 price-index history can additionally provide a gross index reference from the first hypothetical entry session open to the final holdout close. It is attached only on exact equality of observed stock/index dates in that interval, with provider-validated regular-session data and positive revisions; missing evidence remains unavailable. The signed net strategy minus gross price-index return is a descriptive percentage-point difference, not alpha or an executable index investment. The Nifty 50 reference is not a universal venue-specific benchmark or total-return index. Stored history and calendar coverage remain subject to authorized local acceptance and revisions.

Signal-date trend cohorts use the same simulated positions and cached price index. For each signal, require the 61 matching observed dates ending at the signal close. The trailing 60-observation close-to-close Nifty 50 price change is classified as rising (at least +5%), falling (at most -5%) or sideways, otherwise unknown if evidence is missing. No subsequent index close enters classification. Thresholds are illustrative and uncalibrated. Report per-position mean net change and outcome counts for each existing friction scenario, plus classified/total coverage and small-sample flags. Unknown or empty cohorts have no performance estimate. These cohorts are descriptive, not market forecasts, independent samples, full economic regimes or portfolio returns. No new provider call is required.
