# Automatic fund risk and consistency research

The existing AMFI/MFAPI history pipeline is reused, with no new paid provider or portfolio access. Historical results are compared only on the latest recent official AMFI NAV date in the category, using up to 40 sampled Direct Growth schemes. Weekly risk uses 157 target NAV anchors seven days apart, each within three calendar days before the target, with no interpolation or duplicates. Annualized weekly volatility is an approximation; overlapping 52-week windows measure historical consistency, not independent trials. Return-to-drawdown is the positive three-year CAGR divided by observed absolute maximum drawdown of at least 1%. It is not Sharpe, a benchmark-relative measure or a calibrated prediction. No ratio for zero/tiny drawdown or nonpositive CAGR. A sample percentile requires five comparable funds. Index funds may track different assets: no category-wide downside rank or percentile for index funds.

The fund dashboard separates trailing-return and return/drawdown watches with expandable risk details, and the existing recommendation engine now reads the same AMFI coverage through the authenticated internal service route. Overview shows historical watches separately from growth picks. Strict recommendation lists still require official benchmark total-return series, TER, Riskometer, portfolio disclosure and current forward holding evidence; no missing values or ratings are invented. The source remains mixed official AMFI and third-party history with preserved AMFI precedence. No official fund metadata feed or forward-growth prediction has been added. Live authorized data and comparable benchmark validation remain outstanding.


### Weekly risk-adjusted hurdle sensitivity (D02)
Reuse the same 157 dated NAV anchors (156 weekly return proxies), no new network calls. For fixed illustrative effective annual hurdles 0% and 6%, convert to a weekly hurdle via expm1(log1p(h/100)/52), annualize the arithmetic excess mean by 52, use sample standard deviation times sqrt(52), and downside RMS over all 156 intervals times sqrt(52). Sharpe/Sortino proxies use these explicit hypothetical hurdles, not observed risk-free rates. Suppress ratios for annualized denominator below 0.01 percentage points; incomplete/nonfinite history withheld. Backend read model, validated Python per-scheme reports and shared frontend section expose assumptions and values. No new ranking, eligibility, benchmark-hit-rate or tracking gate promotion; actual TRI and observed risk-free series remain dependencies. No new API/provider/schema; synthetic regressions do not establish live data coverage.
