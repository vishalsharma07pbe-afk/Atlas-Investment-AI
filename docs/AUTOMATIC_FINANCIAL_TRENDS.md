# Automatic annual financial trends

Existing authorized company research fetches consolidated yearly income statements, cash flow and balance sheets. The automatic evidence reader now derives revenue, net profit, operating cash flow, total assets and total liabilities comparisons for both public candidates and privately held equity identities. No new provider requests, external AI, credentials, user inputs or storage migration are introduced.

Only explicitly recognizable metric aliases, nonempty reported units, finite numeric values and unambiguous full-year labels (FY2025 or 2025) are accepted. Duplicate metric aliases, duplicate years, future years, ambiguous periods and nonconsecutive latest years produce unavailable comparisons. Growth percentages require a positive prior value and nonnegative latest value; sign changes and negative bases show absolute observations instead. Older reporting periods are marked and omitted from the recent portfolio review digest. The displayed latest period is not the retrieval date.

The portfolio digest raises review prompts for decreases or negative values in revenue, profit and operating cash flow; changes in assets and liabilities remain neutral observations. The company evidence card shows period values, reported units, annual change, direction and missing comparisons. This is not an assessment of fair value, future growth, quality of earnings, debt sustainability or a buy/sell signal. Official filings, reporting-period dates, currency/scale consistency across provider periods and live provider format validation remain necessary. A provider reporting other period labels may yield no trend until its schema is verified.
