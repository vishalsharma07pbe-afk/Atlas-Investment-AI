# Current-session intraday research

The existing Upstox adapter now uses the documented V3 intraday endpoint (https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-intra-day-candle-data/) for completed 15-minute candles. An authorized operator token, entitlement and usage rights are required. The existing exchange calendar gates collection and display. The scheduler selects at most eight mapped cash equities per exchange by prior completed provider daily turnover, requests at most two per exchange per minute, with persistent five-minute cooldown and 15-minute failure cooldown. No user vault, account or order access. V22 is additive.

The authenticated dashboard reads locally stored observations automatically every 30 seconds. At least six contiguous positive-volume bars from the regular-session open are required; unfinished bars, gaps, stale/future bars, raw non-provider sources and closed/unverified sessions are rejected. A 1.5x volume ratio against earlier same-session bars plus a close above prior high and typical-price-weighted VWAP proxy qualifies a descriptive breakout watch. A high above the earlier range followed by a close back within it is a possible failed breakout, not proof of manipulation. Both are bounded heuristics, not executable setups, predictive probabilities, price targets or trade instructions. Order-book liquidity, spread, news, historical time-of-day volume baselines and strategy validation remain missing. No real account was accessed or live performance validated. The Java analysis is computed from automatically collected local bars when the dashboard reads it; there is no persistent intraday publication or forward outcome ledger in this increment.
