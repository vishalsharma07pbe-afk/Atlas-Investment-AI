# Automatic institutional intelligence

Reuses the existing authorized Upstox daily FII/DII NSE cash adapter and the autonomous research scheduler. Compose configures collection by default, subject to the existing usage-rights, entitlement and operator-token gates. One two-endpoint request per hour at most, with persistent claims and retry cooldown; no broker holdings or private data are used. Only bounded, finite nonnegative gross amounts, explicit provider observation timestamps and market scope are stored. Net amounts are recomputed with decimal arithmetic. Future, invalid and conflicting same-timestamp observations are excluded. Later timestamp revisions can update provisional daily observations. History is retained locally for up to 120 days, separately from user-supplied flow records.

The overview and Market Intelligence workspace use the existing research snapshot request to show individual FII/DII observations, source paths and five-date totals only when both participants share the same latest date and at least five matching recent dates. Records older than four calendar days or a collection state older than two hours are not displayed as current. Failed participant refreshes suppress that participant without discarding the other. No units are inferred from undocumented responses; the label remains provider-reported. No aggregate flows are attributed to individual stocks. No external AI, order execution or extra paid provider is introduced.

Existing automatic company evidence already presents shareholding periods. This change does not infer quarter ordering from arbitrary provider labels or invent promoter changes. Authorized bulk/block deal, retail flow, per-stock institutional transaction feeds, independently reconciled ownership and live provider validation remain explicit outstanding evidence. Aggregate observations do not qualify a stock or fund recommendation.
