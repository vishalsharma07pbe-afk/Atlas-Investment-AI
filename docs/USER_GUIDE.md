# ATLAS user guide

## First use

Sign in, follow the three-step onboarding card, and use Help to reopen it. Add holdings from Research Home by selecting the exact listed instrument, entering quantity and average cost, and checking the dated provider price. Import CSV only when you understand its update semantics.

## Understand the labels

- **Live** means the connected provider observation passed the application's freshness checks; it does not guarantee exchange-level real time.
- **Validated daily** means stored end-of-day history passed structural checks.
- **Missing** or **blocked** means ATLAS refused to infer unavailable evidence.
- **Research only** means no broker order can be placed from the feature.
- AI findings are explanations of supplied evidence, not verified facts or personalized advice.

## Research workflow

1. Check Market & Data Health for source, freshness, failures, and quality events.
2. Review portfolio concentration and unpriced positions.
3. Use Stock Explorer or the P2 lab to inspect dated evidence and uncertainty.
4. Treat screener matches and backtests as research filters, not recommendations.
5. Verify important facts against the cited primary source before acting elsewhere.

Keyboard users can press Tab from the top of the page to reveal “Skip to main content.” Dialogs close with Escape where provided. Tables scroll horizontally on small screens, and reduced-motion preferences disable ambient movement.
