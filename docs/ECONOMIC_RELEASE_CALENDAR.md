# Upcoming economic release calendar

Official BLS calendar subscription: https://www.bls.gov/help/hlpiCAL.htm ; feed https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/bls.ics . Explicitly opt in with ATLAS_ECONOMIC_CALENDAR_ENABLED=true and MARKET_DATA_USAGE_RIGHTS_CONFIRMED=true after reviewing applicable terms. Recreate intelligence service. Existing durable coordinator retrieves the fixed feed every six hours; local atomic snapshot, 24-hour capture expiry. Restricted iCalendar subset: standalone VEVENT with stable UID, supported summary, explicit UTC or recognized US Eastern timezone, cancellation excluded, recurrence/ambiguous time/conflicting UIDs rejected. No external calendar links fetched. Scope selected inflation, employment, productivity releases for next 30 days and previous 48 hours; dashboard converts to IST and flags events within 24 hours. Later schedule time never proves publication or actual economic result; RSS evidence remains independent. Reschedules replace prior snapshot, no immutable change history yet. No bell notification, ranking changes, predictions or trade execution. Actual ICS payload compatibility not verified (web reader reports unsupported text/calendar). Fed, ECB, RBI and other calendars, release matching and quantitative surprise research remain pending.
